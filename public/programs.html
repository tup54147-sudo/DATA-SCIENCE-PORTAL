Programs and Admission

B.Sc. (Honours) Data Science — degree structure and admission requirements.

Degree Offered and Duration

The Department offers a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) Honours degree in Data Science.

The minimum period allowed is four (4) academic sessions for UTME candidates and three (3) academic sessions for Direct Entry candidates, while the maximum period allowed is six (6) sessions and one (1) semester for UTME candidates.

Admission Requirements (UTME) Apply

Candidates must obtain at least five O Level credit passes in English Language, Mathematics, Physics, plus two other science subjects in the SSCE (or equivalent) in not more than two sittings. Candidates must also sit for and pass the UTME conducted by JAMB and the post-UTME screening test. UTME subjects required: English Language, Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.

Direct Entry Admission Requirements

To qualify for Direct Entry (DE) admission, candidates must have any of the following, plus the University Matriculation Requirement:

  • Two A Level passes in science subjects including Mathematics; or
  • NCE with at least a merit pass in Mathematics and one other science or social science subject; or
  • Diploma in Data Processing with at least a merit pass from a recognized University, or ND/HND in Data Processing, Engineering, and Mathematics with an overall B grade; or
  • ND/HND (lower credit) in relevant fields and at least NCE merit pass in Mathematics, Statistics, or Computer Science.

Registration and Credit Hours

The minimum number of credit hours a student can register for in any given semester is 24, while the maximum is 15 to 24 depending on standing. SIWES (industrial work experience) runs for four months (one semester) and must be completed in a Data-oriented or computer related industry or establishment.
