About the Department

Our history, philosophy, and what we are equipping our students to do.

Our History

The University of Uyo was established on October 1, 1991, when the Federal Government took over the programmes and staff of the defunct University of Cross River State, Uyo. Today the University comprises 15 faculties, a College of Health Sciences, a Postgraduate School, School of Continuing Education, and a Teaching Hospital.

The Department of Data Science was born on Wednesday, October 4, 2023, during the 128th meeting of the University of Uyo Senate, under the leadership of the then Vice-Chancellor, Professor Nyaudo U. Ndaeyo. The Senate approved the unbundling of the Department of Computer Science, University of Uyo, into five programmes: Computer Science, Cyber Security, Data Science, Information Systems, and Software Engineering.

Dr. Samuel S. Udoh was appointed pioneer Head of Department on December 7, 2023. The administration of the Department gave birth to international standard academic actions aimed to attract and develop senior academics in various fields of Data Analytics, Data Engineering, Computational Intelligence, and Data Warehousing.

Philosophy and Objectives

The philosophy of the B.Sc. programme is to equip Data Science students with the foundations, methods, and implications of data science that extracts knowledge from data using techniques and theories from mathematics, statistics, computing, and information technology.

The objectives of the B.Sc. Data Science programme are to:

  • Provide students with the requisite skills to analyse real life problems that are computationally intensive and independently develop effective solutions;
  • Imbibe students with the skills required to play significant roles in the four A's of data: architecture, acquisition, analysis, and archiving;
  • Train students with the necessary skills to qualify for specialist Data Science jobs in the industry;
  • Provide students with the competency to dig out hidden information in massive data of business, government, healthcare, schools, big data, and other data repositories;
  • Train students on integrating data-driven solutions and business processes to achieve their objectives in an effective and efficient way.

Our Vision

To produce graduates equipped with the analytical, statistical, and computational skills to harness data for innovation, research, and sustainable development across Nigeria and beyond.

Unique Features of the Programme

This curriculum uniquely features an inclusion and emphasis on more hands-on and relevant skills in the areas of data analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and deep learning thereby nurturing a progressive transition from knowledge-based learning to competency-based learning across all levels of study, in recognition of the changing dynamics of computing in the age of digitalization.
